Indian Navy and Sri Lanka Navy Conclude 13th Bilateral Maritime Exercise

The Indian Navy and Sri Lanka Navy have wrapped up the 13th edition of their bilateral maritime exercise, SLINEX-26, held in Visakhapatnam from September 17 to 21. The exercise, conducted under the aegis of the Eastern Naval Command, concluded with the Indian Navy showcasing its indigenous anti-submarine warfare corvette, INS Kavaratti, and fleet tanker, INS Jyoti, while Sri Lanka Navy was represented by SLNS Sindurala.

SLINEX-26 comprised two phases: a Harbour Phase and a Sea Phase. The Harbour Phase was marked by a variety of activities including professional interactions, cross-deck visits, the exchange of best practices, yoga, sporting events, and cultural activities, which involved personnel from both navies.

The Sea Phase focused on coordinated activities at sea to enhance operational synergy and interoperability between the participating forces. Conducted since 2005, SLINEX has become a crucial platform for strengthening maritime cooperation and promoting mutual understanding between the Indian and Sri Lankan navies.

The exercise underscored India’s commitment to a secure, stable, and inclusive maritime environment in line with its MAHASAGAR vision. It also reinforced the enduring maritime partnership between the two countries.

The Ministry of Defence of India highlighted the exercise's importance, noting that it reaffirmed India's dedication to maintaining a secure maritime landscape and enhancing its interoperability with Sri Lanka's navy.
