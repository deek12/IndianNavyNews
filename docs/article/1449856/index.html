India-Sri Lanka Maritime Exercise Concludes Successfully in Visakhapatnam ###

The 13th edition of the India-Sri Lanka bilateral maritime exercise, ‘SLINEX-26’, concluded successfully in Visakhapatnam, India. The exercise, which took place from 17 to 21 September, was a significant event in boosting naval cooperation and interoperability between the two navies.

The exercise included both a harbour phase and a sea phase. The harbour phase was characterized by professional exchanges, cross-deck visits, and sporting interactions between the Indian Navy and the Sri Lanka Navy. These interactions provided a platform for both navies to share best practices and improve their operational capabilities.

The sea phase featured coordinated maritime manoeuvres, air operations, night replenishment at sea, and special forces drills, including Visit, Board, Search and Seizure, and slithering operations. These activities were instrumental in honing the operational coordination and interoperability between the participating forces.

The successful conclusion of ‘SLINEX-26’ reflects the strong maritime partnership between India and Sri Lanka. It underscores their shared commitment to ensuring a secure and stable Indian Ocean region. The exercise is part of India’s vision of MAHASAGAR, a comprehensive maritime security initiative, and their neighbourhood-first policy, which emphasizes close maritime cooperation and capacity building.

The exercise not only enhances interoperability between the Indian and Sri Lanka navies but also contributes to broader regional stability and security. It is a testament to the commitment of both countries to strengthen their maritime security and defense capabilities.
