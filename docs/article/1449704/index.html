INS Trishul Strengthens Indian-Egyptian Naval Relations in Alexandria Port Call

Indian Navy's INS Trishul concluded a three-day port call at Alexandria, Egypt, on September 17, deepening maritime cooperation and enhancing interoperability between the Indian and Egyptian navies. The port call, which was preceded by a Passage Exercise (PASSEX) with the Egyptian Navy, underscored the strategic importance of maintaining close ties between the two navies.

Prior to entering Alexandria harbour, INS Trishul conducted a PASSEX with the Egyptian Naval Ship ENS Bernees. The exercise involved coordinated manoeuvring, communication drills, and approaches for replenishment at sea, facilitating professional exchanges and enhancing interoperability. These interactions provided opportunities for naval personnel from both sides to share best practices and build mutual trust.

Following the PASSEX, INS Trishul was welcomed at the harbour by the Egyptian Naval Ship Al Qadeer. Ambassador Suresh K Reddy, of India's High Commission in Egypt, visited INS Trishul and interacted with its crew. During the visit, Reddy highlighted the significance of the Indian Navy's presence in the region, emphasizing the importance of friendship between India and Egypt.

One of the highlights of the port call was a tribute to Indian soldiers who lost their lives during World War II, conducted at the Chatby War Memorial. The memorial, located in Alexandria, contains 12 graves and bears the names of 19 Indian soldiers who died in naval accidents in the Eastern Mediterranean. This event underscored the enduring bonds of friendship between the two nations.

Throughout the port call, INS Trishul's crew interacted with their Egyptian counterparts, discussing ways to enhance interoperability and exploring avenues for further strengthening maritime cooperation. The port call and the PASSEX further cemented the longstanding relationship between the Indian Navy and the Egyptian Navy, contributing to the broader goal of fostering maritime cooperation between India and Egypt.

The Indian Navy's presence in Alexandria has been a consistent feature of its maritime engagements, reflecting the importance of maintaining strong diplomatic and military ties in the Mediterranean region. Such port calls are essential for deepening mutual understanding and trust, which are crucial for the successful conduct of naval exercises and operations.
