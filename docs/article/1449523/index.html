Indian Navy’s First Next Generation Missile Vessel Keel-Laid at Cochin Shipyard ###

The keel of the Indian Navy’s first Next Generation Missile Vessel (NGMV) was laid at Cochin Shipyard Limited (CSL) in Kochi, marking the formal commencement of construction of the advanced missile vessel project. The keel-laying ceremony was held in the presence of Vice Admiral Sanjay Sadhu, Controller of Warship Production and Acquisition, who was the chief guest. Senior officials from the Navy and CSL also attended the ceremony.

The contract for the construction of the NGMVs was signed with CSL in March 2023. The vessels are designed to carry advanced weapons and sensor systems, aimed at enhancing the Indian Navy’s combat capabilities. The induction of the NGMVs is expected to bolster the Navy’s ability to operate effectively in the Indian Ocean Region, aligning with its objective of maintaining a future-ready and combat-ready force.

The project underscores the growing role of India's domestic defence industry in naval shipbuilding, with all major role-defining equipment planned for the vessels being indigenously developed and manufactured. This is in line with the government's broader push for Aatmanirbhar Bharat, Make in India, and Make for the World, emphasizing a greater focus on reducing dependence on foreign sources for critical defence equipment.

The keel-laying of the first NGMV comes as the Indian Navy continues to expand and modernize its fleet through indigenous warship construction, with Indian shipyards increasingly undertaking the design and construction of sophisticated platforms and combat systems.

In a separate development, Indian Navy NGMV Chief Admiral Krishna Swaminathan recently visited New Zealand, further strengthening maritime cooperation between the Indian Navy and the Royal New Zealand Navy. The two sides discussed enhanced operational engagement, maritime information exchange, and plans for a maiden bilateral naval exercise in 2027.

Simultaneously, the Indian Navy announced that it will commission the second Diving Support Vessel (DSV) of the Nistar-class, Nipun, at the Naval Dockyard in Mumbai on August 31. The vessel is the second in the series and will serve to support diving and underwater operations for the Indian Navy.

These developments reflect the Indian Navy’s commitment to indigenous naval capabilities and its growing partnerships with other maritime forces around the world. The NGMV project and the upcoming bilateral naval exercise underscore the Navy’s commitment to modernization and regional cooperation, reinforcing its position as a key player in the Indian Ocean Region and beyond.
