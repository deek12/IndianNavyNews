Indian Air Force Concludes Joint Exercise with Japan

The Indian Air Force (IAF) and the Japan Air Self-Defense Force (JASDF) wrapped up their joint air defence exercise titled 'Veer Guardian 2026' at Air Force Station Jodhpur in Rajasthan on September 22, 2026. The exercise, which ran for 14 days, aimed to enhance interoperability and strengthen the strategic partnership between the two air forces.

The IAF showcased its fleet, including Su-30 MKI, Rafale, and the indigenous Light Combat Aircraft (LCA) Tejas, while the JASDF participated with its F-2A fighter aircraft. The two sides engaged in a wide range of advanced flying missions, including within visual range combat and coordinated multi-aircraft missions.

During the exercise, notable moments included General Takehiro Morita, Chief of Staff of the JASDF, and Air Chief Marshal AP Singh, Chief of the Air Staff, visiting Air Force Station Jodhpur and flying the Tejas fighter aircraft. Additionally, numerous ground interactions were held, such as subject matter expert exchanges, operational discussions, mission planning conferences, engineering interactions, maintenance practices, and aerospace safety briefings. These interactions allowed both sides to gain valuable insights into each other’s best practices and unique capabilities.

IAF Chief of Staff General Singh highlighted the exercise as a testament to the enduring friendship and professional camaraderie between the two air forces. The event also underscored the ongoing collaboration and strategic depth of the defense partnership between India and Japan.

This joint exercise is expected to further strengthen the mutual defense relationship and build a robust platform for future military cooperation.
