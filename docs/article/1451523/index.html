Varuna 2025: Indian Navy Marks 25th Anniversary of Strategic Partnership with France

The 23rd edition of the Indo-French naval exercise, Varuna, took place between March 19 and 22, 2025, in the waters off the Goa coast of India. This bilateral naval exercise, which celebrates its 25th anniversary this year, aims to enhance the coordination and cooperation between the Indian Navy and the French Navy.

The task group of the French aircraft carrier Charles de Gaulle, including its Rafale fighter aircraft, participated alongside surface ships like FNS Forbin, FNS Provence, and support vessels like FS Marne, along with a nuclear submarine. The Indian Navy also sent its aircraft carrier INS Vikrant, the frigate INS Teg, a Boeing P-8I maritime patrol aircraft, and MiG-29K jets.

The exercise, which marked the 25th anniversary of the strategic partnership, saw participation from both navies. The Indian Navy was represented by INS Chennai, INS Teg, the P-8I aircraft, and an integrated helicopter. The French Navy sent its aircraft carrier Charles de Gaulle, the frigates FS Forbin and Provence, support vessel FS Marne, and a Breguet Atlantique Maritime Patrol Aircraft, as well as several helicopters and amphibious vehicles.

One of the key drills during the Varuna 2025 exercise was the joint anti-submarine warfare (ASW) operation. The Indian submarine played the role of the aggressor, while the Indian and French anti-submarine frigates were deployed to protect a high-value target. The exercise also featured surface warfare maneuvers and replenishment at sea by the fleet tankers of both navies, INS Deepak and Jacques Chevallier.

The Indian Navy's P-8I aircraft, which is operated by the Indian Air Force, made a significant contribution to the exercise. The P-8I, a long-range maritime surveillance and reconnaissance aircraft, demonstrated its capabilities by conducting ASW operations alongside the Indian Navy. This collaboration is indicative of the growing synergy between the two navies and their shared interest in maintaining and improving maritime security in the Indian Ocean region.

The Varuna 2025 exercise also highlighted India and France's commitment to fostering strategic relationships through military cooperation. The event underscored the importance of such alliances in ensuring maritime security and stability in the Indo-Pacific region, where both nations have significant maritime interests.

As the 25th anniversary of Varuna approaches, the exercise serves as a testament to the enduring nature of the strategic partnership between India and France, as well as a model for other nations looking to build and deepen maritime cooperation. The success of Varuna continues to reflect the high level of coordination and mutual trust between the Indian Navy and the French Navy, underpinned by a long-standing history of collaboration and mutual respect.
