DRDO Signs High-Value Agreement with Startup for Quantum Refrigerator Project

The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) has signed a significant high-value deal with a startup company, Zero mK India Private Limited, to develop a 20 mK dilution refrigerator for quantum applications. This first-of-its-kind agreement, part of the Rs 500 crore Technology Development Fund (TDF) scheme, was signed in New Delhi by the Director of the TDF and Zero mK India. The dilution refrigerator, a specialized cryogenic system that operates at extremely low temperatures in the millikelvin range, is crucial for the operation and testing of various quantum computing platforms and related technologies.

This project aims to boost India's indigenous quantum and cryogenic capabilities while reducing the reliance on imported systems. The agreement, highlighted by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, represents the first high-value deal under the recently approved fund aimed at fostering deep-tech and cutting-edge projects in defence technology.

The development of a 20 mK-class dilution refrigerator is an important step towards strengthening India's quantum technology sector and strategic technologies. Aligning with the National Quantum Mission, the project promises to contribute to a self-reliant and quantum-ready ecosystem.

Industry partners, including the DRDO, are expected to benefit from this agreement. The project, monitored and mentored by the Solid State Physics Laboratory (SSPL), will help reduce dependence on imported cryogenic infrastructure and enhance India's domestic quantum technology capabilities.

Defence Secretary, Secretary, Department of Defence R&D, and DRDO Chairman Rajesh Kumar Singh expressed their confidence that the partnership would pave the way for future collaborations in emerging technology domains. Stakeholders from the Solid State Physics Laboratory (SSPL) and various DRDO departments were present at the signing ceremony.

Shivani Sharma, a broadcast journalist specializing in Defence reporting, underscored the significance of this agreement. Her expertise includes reaching the world's highest motorable altitude at Umling La and covering the northernmost strategic point of the country, at Nicobar.
