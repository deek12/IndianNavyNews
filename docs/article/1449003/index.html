Steel Cutting Ceremony for Indian Navy’s Next Generation Missile Vessel Project Held

Cochin Shipyard Limited (CSL) in Kochi recently held a significant ceremony marking the steel cutting for the second Next Generation Missile Vessel (NGMV) project. The event took place on September 17, 2026, attended by Admiral Kapil Mehta, Assistant Controller of Warship Production & Acquisition, who served as the Chief Guest. Other senior officials from the Indian Navy and CSL were also present.

The construction of the NGMVs was finalized with CSL in March 2023, under a contract concluded by the Indian Navy. These ships are expected to be equipped with advanced weaponry and sensors, substantially enhancing the combat capabilities of the Indian Navy to meet future maritime challenges. The project aligns with India's national initiative of "Aatmanirbhar Bharat" and "Make in India," emphasizing the indigenous development of major equipment.

The steel cutting ceremony signifies a significant step in the realization of the NGMV project, reinforcing India's commitment to bolster its naval forces. The ships are designed to operate in the complex maritime environment of the Indian Ocean Region, showcasing the country's technological advancements and strategic vision.

The project underscores India's capability to produce state-of-the-art naval vessels independently, aligning with its broader goals of self-reliance in defense manufacturing and expanding its global influence. The steel cutting ceremony further cements India's resolve to build a robust and modern Navy capable of operating in diverse and challenging conditions.
