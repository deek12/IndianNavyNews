Indian Naval Ships Sahyadri and Kulish Arrive in Philippines for ADMM-Plus JCA

As part of its Operational Deployment, the Indian Navy has dispatched the ships Sahyadri and Kulish to Subic Bay, Philippines. The naval vessels arrived on September 20, 2026, for the ASEAN Defence Ministers' Meeting (ADMM)-Plus Joint Cooperation Activity (JCA). The JCA is a forum that brings together maritime forces from ASEAN Member States and eight Plus countries, facilitating professional and operational engagements aimed at enhancing interoperability, fostering mutual understanding, and strengthening cooperation in the maritime domain.

The participation of Indian naval ships in the ADMM-Plus JCA aligns with the Act East Policy of the Government of India and the vision of MAHASAGAR (Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions). This deployment underscores India’s commitment to multilateral maritime cooperation and its constructive engagement with regional partners.

The Indian Navy's participation in the JCA provides opportunities for naval forces to interact with their counterparts. These engagements are designed to enhance mutual understanding and address shared maritime challenges. The port call also highlights the growing maritime engagement between India and the Philippines, following recent bilateral engagements and the establishment of the India–Philippines Strategic Partnership in August 2025. This deployment serves as an opportunity to further strengthen professional linkages and maritime cooperation between the two countries.
