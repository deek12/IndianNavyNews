India-Mongolia Joint Military Exercise Commences in Uttarakhand

The 18th edition of the India-Mongolia Joint Military Exercise Nomadic Elephant 2026 has commenced in Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand, India. The annual platoon-level exercise, conducted alternately between India and Mongolia, is aimed at enhancing joint military capability to undertake Counter-Insurgency Operations in semi-urban and mountainous terrain under a United Nations mandate. Participating in the exercise are 45 personnel from the Indian Army and the Mongolian Armed Forces.

Nomadic Elephant 2026, which will continue till October 3, focuses on tactical-level operations, exchange of best practices, refinement of operational drills, and strengthening interoperability between the two armies. The training programme will also include Cordon and Search Operations, Room Intervention Drills, Special Heliborne Operations, and Cyber Security Training. The exercise is expected to further strengthen defence cooperation between India and Mongolia and reinforce the longstanding bonds of friendship and mutual trust between the two countries.

The exercise is also expected to cover the use of drones and counter-drone systems, as well as other operational activities. This training will provide personnel from both armies an opportunity to train together in realistic operational scenarios, while refining their tactical procedures and improving interoperability in challenging environments.

The exercise is part of the ongoing India-Mongolia Defence Cooperation, which has seen significant progress in recent years, including the signing of a memorandum of understanding in 2021 aimed at expanding defence ties and cooperation.

The Foreign Training Node in Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand, where the exercise is taking place, is a strategic location for joint military exercises due to its diverse terrain, which includes semi-urban and mountainous areas. The joint military exercise is a testament to the increasing collaboration between India and Mongolia, both of whom are members of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) and have a shared interest in counter-insurgency operations in Central Asia and the Indian Ocean region.
