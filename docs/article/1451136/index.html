NEW DELHI: The Indian Defence Ministry has signed a Rs 810.79 crore contract with Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) to procure 160 Satellite Smart Anti-Airfield Weapons (SAT-SAAW) for the Indian Air Force (IAF). The deal was signed on September 23 in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh in New Delhi, under the Buy (Indian-Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured) category. The SAT-SAAW is an air-to-ground precision-guided glide bomb that can be launched from platforms such as the Jaguar, Hawk, and Su-30 MKI. It has the capability to neutralize enemy airfields from a long stand-off range.

The system, developed and designed by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), will have an indigenous content of 60%. Additionally, the sub-systems like the Initial Measurement Unit, Long Impact Delay Fuse, and Twin Store Carrier will be indigenously developed to further boost the country's self-reliance in defence. The delivery of the SAT-SAAW is scheduled between 2027-2028 and 2028-2029. The integration of the SAT-SAAW will enhance the operational capability of the IAF, ensuring they can continue their unhindered operations while strengthening the country's defence preparedness.
